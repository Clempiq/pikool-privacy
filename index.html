1. Introduction

Welcome to Pikool ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you use our mobile application Pikool (the "App"), available on Google Play.

By using Pikool, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

2. Information We Collect

Pikool does not require you to create an account or provide personal information directly. The App contains no advertising and no advertising SDK.

The following data is collected or generated when you use the App:

  • App-generated device identifier: a random identifier (UUID) created by the App on first launch and stored securely on your device. It is not your advertising ID and cannot be used to identify you personally. We store this identifier on our servers solely to record which content packs you have purchased, so they can be restored on your device.
  • Purchase transaction data: in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Transaction identifiers are handled by RevenueCat and, for individual pack purchases, stored alongside your device identifier on our servers to keep your purchases unlocked.
  • App usage data: basic, anonymous usage events (such as a game being started) may be collected to improve the App.
  • Feedback you send us (optional): from Settings > Help, you can send us a message (a bug, an idea or anything else). It is stored with the App version, your device type and the App-generated device identifier. Your email address is only requested if you want a reply: it is optional and used solely to answer you.
  • Error reports: if the App encounters an error, a technical report is sent automatically (error message, screen concerned, App version and the App-generated device identifier) so that we can fix the problem. It contains no personal data.

This information is stored with our provider Supabase and is never sold or shared with third parties. To request its deletion, contact us at the address below.

Player names entered in-game are stored locally on your device only and are never transmitted to any server.

4. How We Use This Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Process in-app purchases and keep purchased content unlocked
  • Restore your purchases
  • Improve App stability and performance
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

5. Children's Privacy

Pikool is a party drinking game intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

6. Data Retention & Security

Pack-purchase records (device identifier, pack, transaction reference) are retained for as long as needed to keep your purchases unlocked. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our hosting provider in the European Union. In-game player names are stored locally on your device and are removed by uninstalling the App.

You may request the deletion of the purchase records associated with your device identifier at any time by contacting us (see Section 9). Note that deleting these records will permanently remove access to purchased packs.
